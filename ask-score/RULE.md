# Rated questions

Every option offered in the AskUserQuestion tool ends its description with
nine ratings, in this order and form:
`[ease n, convention n, agreement n, intent n, truth n, durability n, integrity n, growth n, evidence n]`.
A hook turns them into the likelihood the user sees that the option is the
best answer. Its weights are not yours to know, and the figure does not
come back to you. The label carries no figure. A question that breaks the
form comes back rejected, with each fault named.

Rate each axis on its own, from 0 to 10, against its anchors. 5 is the
middle of the scale, not a poor mark.

First the pull an option has apart from its merit. These axes give
cheapness, habit, and the wish to agree a place of their own, so that they
stay out of the merit below. A rating here measures how much pull the
option has; it is no verdict on the option.

- ease: how cheap, fast, and small the option is to carry out. 0 a large,
  slow change; 10 a few lines.
- convention: how standard it is, and how easy to defend in a meeting.
  0 needs a case made; 10 what everyone does.
- agreement: how far it sides with what the user said or leans toward.
  0 against that lean; 10 exactly that lean.

Then its merit:

- intent: how fully it reaches the goal behind the user's words. 0 misses
  it; 10 the goal itself.
- truth: how true it is to the nature of the problem. 0 treats a symptom
  or forces a foreign shape; 10 meets the cause in the problem's own terms.
- durability: how well it holds over time and under failure. 0 breaks on
  the next change; 10 still right in ten years without rework.
- integrity: how coherent and honest its construction is. 0 a patch that
  hides what it does; 10 one idea throughout, nothing bolted on.
- growth: how far it opens future possibilities. 0 a dead end; 10 opens
  doors and invites the next step.

Last, the ground the merit stands on:

- evidence: how much of the merit ratings rests on what this session
  verified. 0 guessed; 5 read in the code or the docs; 10 read and ran.

An axis outside the question, such as truth when the question is which
file the user meant, is `–` for every option alike. intent and evidence
take a rating in every question. A question asked in prose carries no
figure.
